What affects the price

  • Type of shoot and how long it runs
  • How soon you need the photographer
  • Number of edited photos and how fast you need them
  • Travel to more than one venue, or outside the city
  • The photographer's experience and portfolio

Frequently asked questions

How much does a photographer cost in India?

It depends on the type and length of the shoot. On SnapPro, 2-hour guide prices start around ₹2,200 for candid photos, ₹2,500 for portraits, ₹3,500 for birthdays, ₹4,000 for events, ₹5,000 for products, ₹7,000 for corporate and ₹12,000 for weddings. Each photographer sends their own quote.

How much does a wedding photographer cost in India?

On SnapPro the guide price is about ₹12,000 for 2 hours and ₹36,000 for 6 hours of a single function. Multi-day weddings, a second photographer, videography and albums are quoted separately by each photographer.

Why do same-day bookings cost more?

Photographers need to drop other plans to reach you quickly. On SnapPro, 'right now' requests are suggested at about 60% more, 'in 1 hour' about 35% more and 'in 3 hours' about 15% more than a planned shoot.

Is there a booking fee?

No. Customers pay only the photographer's quoted price, online through Razorpay — 25% to book and the rest after the shoot starts.
