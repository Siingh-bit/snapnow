How to book

  1. Post your event shoot — date, time, how long, the area and your budget. It takes about a minute.
  2. Get quotes from verified photographers who do this kind of work. Each one sends their own price.
  3. Compare and book — look at portfolios, ratings and prices, then pay a 25% advance online to confirm.
  4. Shoot, then pay the rest — chat or call your photographer in the app, and pay the balance once the shoot starts.

Tips

  • Share the event schedule so key moments aren't missed.
  • Say which guests or families must be photographed.
  • Ask whether lighting at the venue is good enough or if they'll bring flash.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Have you shot events of this size before?
  • How many edited photos will we get?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a event photographer cost in India?

On SnapPro, each event photographer sets their own price. SnapPro's guide price is about ₹4,000 for a 2-hour shoot and ₹6,000 for 3 hours, booked a day or more ahead.

How do I hire a event photographer?

Post your event shoot on snappro.in with the date, time, city and budget. Verified event photographers near you send quotes; compare and book online.

What does event photography include?

It typically covers parties, anniversaries, engagements, poojas, college fests, launches and get-togethers. Agree on the number of edited photos and the delivery time with your photographer.

What should I ask a event photographer?

Have you shot events of this size before? How many edited photos will we get?

Are photographers on SnapPro verified?

Yes. Every photographer is reviewed by the SnapPro team before they can quote, and photographers verify their identity with a government ID and a face photo. Ratings come only from customers who completed a booking.
