Popular places for pre-wedding photos in Rishikesh

Photographers in Rishikesh often shoot at the Ganga ghats and the area around Laxman Jhula. Some public places and monuments need a photography permit or charge a camera fee, and many get crowded later in the day — ask your photographer which spots work best and plan for early-morning or golden-hour light.

Tips for a great pre-wedding shoot

  • Pick 2–3 locations close together so you don't lose light travelling.
  • Book early-morning or evening slots for soft light and fewer crowds.
  • Check if the location needs a photography permit.
  • Bring an outfit change and comfortable shoes.

Shot ideas

  • A walk through an old-city lane or heritage site
  • Beach or lakeside shots at sunset
  • A café or rooftop at night with fairy lights
  • Recreating how you met
