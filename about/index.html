About SnapPro

SnapPro at a glanceSnapPro (snappro.in) is an Indian online marketplace for booking professional photographers. Customers post a shoot and verified photographers in their city send quotes. Bookings are paid online through Razorpay (25% advance, balance after the shoot starts). SnapPro covers weddings, pre-wedding shoots, birthdays, events, corporate, product, food, real estate, maternity, fashion, pet and candid photography across India.

What we do

Finding a good photographer usually means asking around, scrolling through profiles and negotiating on the phone. SnapPro flips that: you describe your shoot once, and photographers who do that kind of work in your city come to you with their price. You compare their work and reviews and book in a few taps.

How we keep it trustworthy

  • Photographers are approved by our team after a portfolio review, and verify their identity with a government ID and face photo.
  • Ratings come only from completed bookings.
  • Payments are handled by Razorpay; SnapPro never sees card numbers or UPI PINs.
  • Our support team can step in if something goes wrong.
