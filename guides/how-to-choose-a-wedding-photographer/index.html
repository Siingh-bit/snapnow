How to Choose a Wedding Photographer in India

A step-by-step checklist to choose the right wedding photographer in India — portfolios, style, questions to ask, contracts and budgets.

In shortStart with full galleries, not highlights. Decide on the style you want. Match the team to the size of your wedding. Agree on deliverables in writing.

1. Start with full galleries, not highlights

Highlights show a photographer's best 20 frames. Ask for two or three complete wedding galleries — that shows how they handle low light at the reception, crowded rituals and family groups.

2. Decide on the style you want

Traditional photography covers rituals and family groups; candid photography captures natural moments. Most couples want both — check whether one person can do both or whether you need two.

3. Match the team to the size of your wedding

For large guest lists and functions running at the same time, ask whether a second shooter is included.

4. Agree on deliverables in writing

Number of edited photos, delivery time, raw files, albums and any travel charges should be written down before you pay.

5. Plan the day together

Share your function timeline, venues, and a list of must-have family groups so nothing is missed.

6. Budget sensibly

On SnapPro, a 6-hour wedding function has a guide price of about ₹36,000; each photographer quotes their own price. Book early for peak wedding season dates.
