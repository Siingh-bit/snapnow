1. What SnapPro does

SnapPro is a marketplace. Customers post photography shoots; independent photographers send quotes; customers choose and book one. Photographers are independent professionals, not SnapPro employees. SnapPro provides the platform, takes payment for bookings and pays photographers their share.

2. Accounts

3. Quotes, bookings and prices

  • Each photographer sets their own price in their quote. The price shown when you book is the total you pay — there is no separate booking fee for customers.
  • You pay in two parts, both online through SnapPro: an advance of 25% of the price when you book, and the balance (75%) once the shoot has started. The exact amounts are shown before you pay.
  • A booking is confirmed only when the advance has been received and verified. The balance is due at the shoot and must be paid in the app; please don't pay the photographer in cash.
  • SnapPro keeps a commission from each booking (shown to photographers before they quote) and pays the rest to the photographer once the shoot is complete and the balance has been paid.

Payments are processed by Razorpay, a licensed payment aggregator. You can pay with UPI, debit or credit cards, or net banking. SnapPro does not see or store your card number, CVV or UPI PIN. Prices are in Indian Rupees (₹).
