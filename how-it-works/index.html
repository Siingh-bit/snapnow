How SnapPro works

SnapPro is the simple way to book a professional photographer in India. You don't need to search, call or negotiate — photographers come to you with their price.

For customers

  1. Post your shoot — date, time, how long, the area and your budget. It takes about a minute.
  2. Get quotes from verified photographers who do this kind of work. Each one sends their own price.
  3. Compare and book — look at portfolios, ratings and prices, then pay a 25% advance online to confirm.
  4. Shoot, then pay the rest — chat or call your photographer in the app, and pay the balance once the shoot starts.

Why people book on SnapPro

  • Photographers are approved by our team after a portfolio review, and verify their identity with a government ID and face photo.
  • Real ratings — only customers who completed a booking can leave a review.
  • Clear prices — each photographer quotes upfront, with no booking fee for customers.
  • Secure payments with Razorpay — UPI, cards and net banking; 25% to book, the rest after the shoot starts.
  • Chat and call your photographer inside the app; your exact address is shared only with the photographer you book.
  • Fair cancellations — full advance back if you cancel before your photographer sets off.

For photographers

Join free, add your portfolio, verify your ID and start quoting on requests in your city. Customers pay online; SnapPro sends your share to your UPI ID after the shoot. Learn more.

Frequently asked questions

What is SnapPro?

SnapPro (snappro.in) is an online marketplace in India for booking professional photographers. You post what you need — the type of shoot, date, time, city and budget — and verified photographers in your city send you their own price. You compare their portfolios, ratings and quotes, book the one you like and pay securely online.

How do I book a photographer on SnapPro?

Sign up at snappro.in, post your shoot (it takes about a minute), and photographers who shoot that kind of work in your city send quotes. Compare them, tap Book, and pay a 25% advance to confirm. The rest is paid in the app once the shoot starts.

How much does it cost to book a photographer on SnapPro?

Each photographer sets their own price in their quote, and there is no booking fee for customers. As a starting guide, a 2-hour shoot on SnapPro is suggested at about ₹2,200 for candid, ₹2,500 for portraits, ₹3,500 for birthdays, ₹4,000 for events, ₹7,000 for corporate and ₹12,000 for weddings — longer shoots and same-day bookings cost more.

Are photographers on SnapPro verified?

Yes. Every photographer is reviewed by the SnapPro team before they can quote, and photographers verify their identity with a government ID and a face photo. Ratings come only from customers who completed a booking.

How does payment work?

You pay online through Razorpay — UPI, Google Pay, PhonePe, cards or net banking. You pay a 25% advance to confirm the booking and the balance in the app once the shoot starts. SnapPro pays the photographer after the shoot.

Can I cancel a booking?

Yes. If you cancel before your photographer sets off, your advance is refunded in full. If you cancel after they've set off, half of the advance is refunded. See the cancellation and refund policy for details.
