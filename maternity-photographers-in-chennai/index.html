Popular places for maternity photos in Chennai

Photographers in Chennai often shoot at Marina Beach, Elliot's Beach in Besant Nagar and Mahabalipuram (a short drive away). Some public places and monuments need a photography permit or charge a camera fee, and many get crowded later in the day — ask your photographer which spots work best and plan for early-morning or golden-hour light.

Tips for a great maternity shoot

  • Most people shoot between 28 and 34 weeks, when the bump shows and you're still comfortable — check with your doctor.
  • Choose a time of day when you have the most energy.
  • Keep a chair, water and snacks nearby.

Shot ideas
