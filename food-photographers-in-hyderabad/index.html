Shooting in Hyderabad

Food shoots usually happen at your home, office, venue or a studio, so location matters less than light and preparation. If you'd like an outdoor touch, well-known spots in Hyderabad include Golconda Fort, Charminar, Necklace Road by Hussain Sagar — ask your photographer whether they need a permit.

Tips for a great food shoot

  • Shoot when the kitchen can plate each dish fresh.
  • Shoot near a window or ask the photographer to bring lights.
  • Prioritise best-sellers and new dishes.

Shot ideas

  • Top-down flat-lays of thalis and platters
  • Steam and pour action shots
  • The dining room at its busiest
