The key points, in plain words. You accept each of these separately in the SnapPro app. The full agreement below is what applies.

  3. I will do each booking myself and will not send someone else in my place without approval. (Clause 4.4)
  4. Cancellation, no-show and late-delivery fees in Schedule 2 apply and can be deducted from my payouts. (Clause 6, Sch. 2)

  10. I will use customer data only for the booking, keep it safe, and delete it as set out in Schedule 4. (Clause 12, Sch. 4)

  12. I indemnify SnapPro for claims arising from my work, and SnapPro's liability to me is capped (but my earned payouts are never capped). (Clause 16.2, 17.4, 17.5)
  13. SnapPro may change these terms or the standard Commission with 30 days' notice; I can leave before a change takes effect. (Clause 18.2)
  14. Disputes go to arbitration seated in Mumbai. (Clause 20.3)

1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1
In this Agreement, unless the context requires otherwise, the following words have the meanings given below:

“Advance” means the portion of the Booking Price payable by the Customer at the time of confirming a Booking, as displayed on the Platform from time to time.

“Balance” means the Booking Price less the Advance and any amount already paid.

“Booking” means a confirmed engagement between a Customer and the Photographer created on the Platform when the Customer accepts the Photographer's Quote and the Advance is received by SnapPro.

“Booking Content” means all photographs, videos, edits and other material created by the Photographer in the course of a Booking.

“Booking Price” means the total price for a Booking as quoted by the Photographer and accepted by the Customer on the Platform, before any discount, credit or reward funded by SnapPro.

“Business Day” means a day other than a Sunday or a public holiday in Mumbai.

“Commission” means the fee payable by the Photographer to SnapPro for each Booking, calculated as set out in Clause 7 and Schedule 1.

“Customer Data” means any information relating to an identified or identifiable Customer or any person at a shoot, including names, phone numbers, addresses, images and likeness, received or created by the Photographer in connection with the Platform.

“Introduced Customer” means any Customer with whom the Photographer first came into contact (by quotation, chat, call or Booking) through the Platform.

“Payable Amount” means the Photographer's Share that has become payable under Clause 8.1, after any deduction, hold or set-off permitted by this Agreement.

“Photographer's Share” means for each payment received by SnapPro from a Customer for a Booking, that payment less the Commission attributable to it, and less any Taxes required by law to be deducted or collected.

“Platform” means the SnapPro website, mobile applications, partner tools, chat, calling and payment features and related services, as modified from time to time.

“Platform Policies” means the Schedules to this Agreement and the policies, guidelines and rules published by SnapPro on the Platform from time to time for photographers, including the Privacy Policy, Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy.

“Portfolio Content” means photographs, videos, text and other material uploaded by the Photographer to the Platform, including to the Photographer's profile and portfolio.

“Quote” means a price and proposal sent by the Photographer to a Customer through the Platform.

“Taxes” means all taxes, duties, levies, cesses and withholdings, including goods and services tax (GST), tax deducted at source (TDS) and tax collected at source (TCS).

1.2
In this Agreement: (a) headings are for convenience only and do not affect interpretation; (b) the words “including” and “includes” are illustrative and do not limit the words before them; (c) references to a statute include any amendment, re-enactment or replacement of it; (d) the singular includes the plural and vice versa; and (e) references to Clauses and Schedules are to clauses of and schedules to this Agreement, and the Schedules form part of this Agreement.
1.3
If there is a conflict between the body of this Agreement and a Schedule, the body of this Agreement prevails, except that Schedule 1 (Commercial Terms) prevails on any matter of Commission or payout. If there is a conflict between this Agreement and any other Platform Policy, this Agreement prevails.

2.2
Independent professional. The Photographer is an independent contractor carrying on his or her own business. Nothing in this Agreement creates any relationship of employment, partnership, joint venture, franchise or agency between the Parties. The Photographer: (a) decides whether to accept any request and what price to quote; (b) decides how to perform the services, using his or her own skill, equipment and methods; (c) is free to work through other channels and platforms, subject to Clause 10; and (d) has no authority to bind SnapPro or to make any representation on its behalf.
2.3
The Photographer is solely responsible for his or her own Taxes, insurance, equipment, travel, assistants and statutory compliances. Any assistant or second shooter engaged by the Photographer is the Photographer's sole responsibility, and the Photographer shall ensure their compliance with this Agreement as if they were the Photographer.
2.4
Contract for services. The Customer contracts with the Photographer for the photography services. SnapPro provides the Platform, facilitates the Booking, and collects payments from the Customer as the limited agent of the Photographer for the sole purpose of receiving payment, as further set out in Clause 7.
2.5
No minimum business. SnapPro does not guarantee any minimum number of requests, Bookings, visibility, ranking or earnings. The order in which photographers are shown to Customers is determined by SnapPro's systems, which may consider distance, availability, ratings, reliability, response time, verification status, price and other factors, and may change from time to time.
2.6
Social security registration. Where SnapPro is required by applicable law, including the Code on Social Security, 2020 and rules under it, to register, report or make contributions in respect of persons using the Platform, the Photographer authorises SnapPro to share the information reasonably required for that purpose with the relevant authority or portal, and agrees to cooperate with any such registration.

3. Onboarding, verification and account

3.1
The Photographer shall be at least eighteen (18) years of age and legally competent to contract, and shall provide true, accurate, current and complete information during onboarding and at all times thereafter, including name, contact details, city, service areas, PAN, and payout details.
3.2
Verification. The Photographer consents to SnapPro verifying his or her identity, including by collecting a government-issued identity document and a live face photograph, and by conducting reasonable checks with third-party service providers. SnapPro may decline, delay or reverse approval of any profile in its reasonable discretion, including where verification is incomplete or inconsistent.
3.3
The Photographer shall keep login credentials confidential, shall not share or transfer the account, and shall not maintain more than one account. The Photographer is responsible for all activity carried out through his or her account.
3.4
Payout details. The Photographer shall provide and keep updated a UPI ID or bank account held in the Photographer's own name (or the name of the Photographer's firm). SnapPro is entitled to rely on the payout details last provided, and a payment made to those details shall be a complete discharge of SnapPro's obligation for that amount.

4. Quotes and bookings

4.1
A Quote sent by the Photographer is an offer to the Customer to provide the services described, at the price stated, on the date and time and at the location stated in the Customer's request. A Booking is formed when the Customer accepts the Quote and the Advance is received by SnapPro. A Quote may not be withdrawn after it has been accepted by the Customer.
4.2
Each Quote shall state clearly what is included (such as hours, number of edited photographs, delivery format and timeline, travel and any extras). The Photographer shall not charge the Customer any amount over and above the Booking Price for anything that was stated in the Quote or in the Customer's request, and any additional work agreed during a shoot shall be added and paid through the Platform where the Platform allows it.
4.3
All payments through the Platform. The Photographer shall not request, accept or arrange any payment from a Customer for a Booking (including cash, UPI or bank transfer, tips presented as fees, or payment for “extras”) otherwise than through the Platform, unless SnapPro expressly permits it in writing.
4.4
Personal performance. The Photographer shall perform each Booking personally. The Photographer shall not send any other person in his or her place, or subcontract a Booking, without the prior approval of SnapPro and the Customer through the Platform.
4.5
The Photographer shall keep his or her availability, busy times and service areas accurate on the Platform, and shall not quote for a date and time on which he or she is not available.

5. Service standards

5.1
The Photographer shall perform each Booking with the skill, care and diligence expected of a competent professional photographer, in accordance with the Quote, the Customer's reasonable instructions, Schedule 2 (Cancellations, No-Shows and Service Levels) and Schedule 3 (Code of Conduct).
5.2
The Photographer shall arrive at the location at the booked time, update the Booking status on the Platform (on the way, arrived, shoot started, shoot completed) at the relevant time, and use the Platform's chat and calling features for communications about the Booking wherever reasonably possible.
5.3
Delivery. The Photographer shall deliver the Booking Content agreed in the Quote by the delivery date stated in the Quote or, if none is stated, within seven (7) days after the shoot, in a usable digital format and by a secure method, and shall retain a backup copy for at least thirty (30) days after delivery.
5.4
The Photographer shall promptly and courteously address any reasonable complaint by a Customer and shall cooperate with SnapPro in resolving any dispute, including by providing evidence such as delivery links, timestamps and communications.

6. Cancellations and no-shows

6.1
The Photographer acknowledges that a cancellation or failure to attend by the Photographer causes loss to the Customer and to SnapPro, including the cost of refunds, re-arranging another photographer at short notice, customer goodwill and harm to SnapPro's reputation, which are difficult to quantify precisely.
6.2
Accordingly, if the Photographer cancels a Booking, fails to attend, attends materially late, or fails to deliver, the Photographer shall pay SnapPro the fees set out in Schedule 2, which the Parties agree are a genuine pre-estimate of the loss likely to be suffered and are not a penalty. SnapPro may recover such fees under Clause 8.
6.3
Fees under Schedule 2 shall not apply where the cancellation or failure is caused by a Force Majeure Event under Clause 19, or by a genuine medical emergency of the Photographer or an immediate family member, provided the Photographer informs the Customer and SnapPro through the Platform as soon as possible and provides reasonable supporting evidence on request.
6.4
Repeated cancellations, no-shows or late deliveries may result in reduced visibility, suspension or termination under Clause 15, in addition to the fees in Schedule 2.
6.5
Cancellations by Customers are governed by SnapPro's Refund & Cancellation Policy as published on the Platform. Where a Customer cancels and the Advance or part of it is retained under that policy, the Photographer's Share of the amount retained shall be paid to the Photographer in accordance with Clause 8.

7. Fees, commission and collection of payments

7.1
Commission. For every Booking, the Photographer shall pay SnapPro a Commission equal to the percentage of the Booking Price set out in Schedule 1. Unless Schedule 1 specifies a special rate for the Photographer, the Commission is the standard rate displayed to the Photographer on the Platform at the time the Quote is sent.
7.2
The Commission applicable to a Booking is fixed at the time the Customer accepts the Quote, and a later change in the Commission does not affect a Booking already made.
7.3
The Commission is exclusive of GST. Where GST is applicable on the Commission, it shall be charged in addition and deducted from the Photographer's Share, and SnapPro shall issue the corresponding tax invoice where required by law.
7.4
Collection. The Photographer appoints SnapPro as his or her limited agent to receive, through SnapPro's payment service providers, all amounts paid by Customers for Bookings. A payment by a Customer to SnapPro for a Booking shall, as between the Customer and the Photographer, discharge the Customer's obligation to pay that amount to the Photographer.
7.5
The Advance and the Balance shall be collected from the Customer in the proportions and at the times displayed on the Platform from time to time. The Commission is first adjusted from the Advance, and the remainder of the Advance and the entire Balance form part of the Photographer's Share.
7.6
Discounts, offers, credits and rewards given to Customers at SnapPro's initiative are funded by SnapPro and do not reduce the Photographer's Share, unless the Photographer has agreed to participate in a specific promotion through the Platform.
7.7
Payment gateway charges on Customer payments are borne by SnapPro and are not deducted from the Photographer's Share, unless SnapPro gives thirty (30) days' notice of a change under Clause 18.

8. Payouts, holds, set-off and recovery

8.1
When money becomes payable. The Photographer's Share of each payment received by SnapPro from a Customer becomes payable to the Photographer when that payment is successfully received and confirmed by SnapPro's payment service provider, subject to this Clause 8.
8.2
Payout cycle. SnapPro shall remit the Payable Amount to the Photographer's payout details in accordance with its payout cycle as displayed on the Platform, which shall ordinarily not exceed seven (7) Business Days from the date the amount becomes payable. Each payout shall carry a unique payout number and payment reference, and the Photographer will be able to view on the Platform the Bookings and payments covered by it.
8.3
Holds. SnapPro may withhold the whole or part of any Payable Amount, for such period as is reasonably necessary, where: (a) a Customer has raised a complaint, dispute, chargeback or refund request concerning a Booking; (b) SnapPro reasonably suspects fraud, misuse, a breach of this Agreement or a safety concern; (c) the Photographer's payout details or verification are incomplete or under review; (d) Booking Content has not been delivered by the delivery date; or (e) SnapPro is required to do so by law or by any authority. SnapPro shall inform the Photographer of the hold through the Platform and release the withheld amount, less any amount properly due to SnapPro or the Customer, once the matter is resolved.
8.4
Set-off. SnapPro may deduct from, and set off against, any amount payable to the Photographer under this Agreement any amount owed by the Photographer to SnapPro or to a Customer, including: fees under Schedule 2; refunds made to Customers for a Booking (including after the Photographer's Share for that Booking has been paid out); chargebacks; amounts paid to the Photographer in error; Taxes; and amounts due under Clauses 10 and 16.
8.5
Recovery. If the amounts available for set-off are insufficient, the Photographer shall pay the shortfall to SnapPro within seven (7) days of a written demand (including through the Platform). Until paid, SnapPro may also deduct the shortfall from future payouts.
8.6
Refunds after payout. Where a Customer is refunded, in whole or in part, for a payment whose Photographer's Share has already been paid out, the corresponding Photographer's Share (in the same proportion as the refund) becomes recoverable from the Photographer under Clauses 8.4 and 8.5.
8.7
Statements. The Photographer shall review payouts and statements on the Platform and notify SnapPro of any error within sixty (60) days of the payout date, after which the payout and statement shall be deemed correct, except in the case of fraud or manifest error.
8.8
No interest is payable on any amount withheld or paid in accordance with this Clause 8.

SnapPro shall deposit the amounts so deducted or collected and make the corresponding certificates and statements available as required by law.
9.3
The Photographer shall provide a valid PAN and, where registered, GSTIN, and shall notify SnapPro of any change. If the Photographer fails to do so, SnapPro may deduct Taxes at the higher rates prescribed by law, and such deduction shall not be refundable by SnapPro.

The Photographer remains free to serve any person who did not come to the Photographer through the Platform, and any Introduced Customer through the Platform.

Termination for cause. SnapPro may terminate this Agreement with immediate effect by notice if the Photographer: (a) commits a material breach that is incapable of remedy or, if capable of remedy, is not remedied within seven (7) days of notice; (b) breaches Clause 4.3, Clause 10, Clause 12 or Schedule 3; (c) commits fraud or provides false information; or (d) is the subject of a credible allegation of a criminal offence connected with the Platform or any Booking.
