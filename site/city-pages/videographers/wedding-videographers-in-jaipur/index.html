Filming in Jaipur

For pre-wedding stories and couple shots, videographers in Jaipur often film at Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, Nahargarh Fort, Patrika Gate. Venues and monuments may need permission to film. Drone shots need permission in many places in India and are not allowed near airports, military areas and many monuments. Ask your videographer whether they are registered to fly and whether your venue allows it.

Tips for your wedding video

  • Share the full function timeline so the videographer can plan where to stand for each ritual.
  • Tell them which moments must be filmed without cuts — vows, pheras, speeches.
  • Ask how long the highlight film and the full video will be, and when each will be ready.
  • If you also want photos, choose Photos + video so one person or team plans both together.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding videographer cost in Jaipur?

Photographers in Jaipur set their own prices on SnapPro. As a guide, a 2-hour wedding video is about ₹15,600 and a 6-hour booking about ₹46,800; photos and video together are about ₹21,600 for 2 hours. Multi-day functions, drone, extra editing and albums are quoted separately.

How do I book a wedding videographer in Jaipur?

Sign up at snappro.in, post your wedding shoot with the dates and venue in Jaipur, and choose Video or Photos + video. Compare the quotes you receive and book with a 25% advance online.

Can one person do both photos and video for my wedding in Jaipur?

Yes — choose Photos + video and only photographers who do both will quote. For large events, ask whether they bring a second person so neither photos nor video miss a moment.

What should I ask a wedding videographer?

Can I see two complete wedding films you've made, not just a teaser? How long will the highlight film and the full video be, and when will I get them? Do you record clean audio for vows and speeches? Is drone footage possible at my venue, and are you registered to fly?

Can I see their camera and gear?

Every photographer on SnapPro lists their gear — camera body and model, lenses, gimbal, lights, microphone and drone, or the phone model if they shoot on a phone. It's on their profile and next to every quote, and you can filter by Pro camera or Has drone.

Are drone shots allowed?

Drone shots need permission in many places in India and are not allowed near airports, military areas and many monuments. Ask your videographer whether they are registered to fly and whether your venue allows it.
