How to book

  1. Post your event shoot and choose Video — or Photos + video if you want both. Add the date, time, how long, the area and your budget.
  2. Get quotes from verified photographers who shoot video. Each one sends their own price and lists their gear — camera, lenses, gimbal, microphone, drone.
  3. Compare and book — watch their work, check their gear and ratings, then pay a 25% advance online to confirm.
  4. Shoot, then pay the rest — plan the day in the in-app chat or call, and pay the balance once the shoot starts.

Tips

  • Share the run-of-show so key moments aren't missed.
  • Tell them if speeches or performances need clear sound.
  • Agree whether you want a short highlight, the full recording, or both.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Will you record sound from the mic or stage?
  • How soon can you share a short highlight for social media?

Gear and drones

Every photographer on SnapPro lists their gear — camera body and model, lenses, gimbal, lights, microphone and drone, or the phone model if they shoot on a phone. It's on their profile and next to every quote, and you can filter by Pro camera or Has drone.

Drone shots need permission in many places in India and are not allowed near airports, military areas and many monuments. Ask your videographer whether they are registered to fly and whether your venue allows it.
