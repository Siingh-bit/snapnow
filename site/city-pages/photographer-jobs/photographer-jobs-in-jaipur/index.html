How it works for photographers in Jaipur

  1. Create your account — your name, mobile number, the types of shoot you do and your city. It takes about two minutes.
  2. Get approved — add at least 6 photos of your own work and verify your identity (front and back of a government ID and a face photo). The SnapPro team reviews your profile.
  3. Send your price on shoot requests from customers in Jaipur. You see the type of shoot, date, area and the customer's budget before you quote. Before your first quote you add your UPI ID, list your gear and accept the Partner Agreement.
  4. Shoot and get paid — the customer pays a 25% advance online when they book and the balance once the shoot starts. SnapPro sends your share to your UPI ID after the shoot.

What you need

  • At least 6 photos or videos of your own work for your portfolio
  • A government ID (Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or passport) and a face photo
  • A camera or a phone — both are welcome. You list what you shoot with, and customers see it before they book
  • A UPI ID to receive your share
  • You choose what you offer: photos, video or both
  • You choose how far you travel: only your city, a distance in km, your whole state or anywhere in India

Where shoots happen in Jaipur

Most bookings are at the customer's home, venue or office. For outdoor portraits, couple shoots and pre-wedding work, customers in Jaipur often ask for Amer Fort, Hawa Mahal, Nahargarh Fort, Patrika Gate. Some public places and monuments need a photography permit or charge a camera fee — check before the day.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get photography clients in Jaipur?

Join SnapPro free at snappro.in, add your portfolio and verify your ID. Once approved you see shoot requests posted by customers in Jaipur — or as far as you choose to travel — and send your own price. The customer compares quotes and books online.

Are these full-time photographer jobs in Jaipur?

No. This is freelance work: customers post individual shoots and you choose which ones to quote on. There is no salary, no fixed hours and no minimum number of shoots.

How much can I earn?

You set your own price on every quote. As a guide, customers on SnapPro are shown suggested prices of about ₹3,500 for a 2-hour birthday shoot, ₹4,000 for an event, ₹7,000 for corporate work and ₹12,000 for a wedding function; video is suggested at about 1.3 times the photo price. SnapPro's commission is taken from the booking amount, and the rate is shown before you send any quote.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free and there are no listing fees or charges per lead. SnapPro keeps a commission only on bookings you complete. Every photographer gets 250 MB of portfolio space free.

Can videographers join?

Yes. In your profile choose whether you shoot photos, video or both. Customers pick what they need when they post a shoot, and you get the requests that match.

Can I join if I shoot on a phone?

Yes. You list your phone model and customers see it on your profile and next to your quote. Some customers ask for a professional camera only; those requests go to photographers who list a camera.

How and when do I get paid?

Customers pay online. After the shoot is complete and fully paid, SnapPro sends your share to the UPI ID you add in the app.
