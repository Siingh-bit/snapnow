SnapPro website files, grouped for people. The published web addresses don't change:
tools/build_site.py lays everything out flat when you push (see ../README.md).

  main/         home page, app, admin, service worker, app manifest
  seo-and-ai/   sitemap, robots.txt, llms.txt (for AI assistants), IndexNow key
  images/       icons and share image
  legal/        terms, privacy, refunds, contact, partner agreement ...
  pages/        about, FAQ, guides, how it works, prices, for photographers
  city-pages/   one folder per shoot type, each holding that type's city pages
