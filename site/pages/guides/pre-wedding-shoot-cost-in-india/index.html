What changes the price

  • Number of locations — travelling between spots takes time, and each one adds to the hours
  • Outfit changes and themes — props, styling or a planned theme take longer
  • Destination shoots — travel and stay for the photographer outside the city
  • Video or a short film — a pre-wedding film costs more than photos alone
  • Permits and entry fees for monuments and private venues

What's usually included

  • Planning the look and locations with you
  • Couple portraits in two or three outfits
  • Candid and story shots
  • A set of edited photos (agree the number in advance)

How to save

  • Book for a later date — same-day requests cost more.
  • Keep the hours realistic: pre-wedding shoots usually need about 4 hours.
  • Choose only what you need: photos, video, or both.
  • Compare a few quotes — on SnapPro photographers in your city send their own prices.
  • Agree on deliverables in writing so there are no surprise extras.

Questions to ask before you book

  • Which locations in the city do you recommend and do any need permits?
  • How many outfit changes can we do?
  • Do you help with poses and ideas?

What is included in a pre-wedding shoot?

Usually: planning the look and locations with you; couple portraits in two or three outfits; candid and story shots; a set of edited photos (agree the number in advance). Agree on the number of edited photos and the delivery time before you book.

Why do pre-wedding photographers charge different prices?

Experience, gear, editing time, travel and how busy the date is. Number of locations — travelling between spots takes time, and each one adds to the hours.

Is it cheaper to book a pre-wedding photographer in advance?

Yes. On SnapPro, shoots booked for a later date have the lowest guide prices; asking for a photographer right now costs about 60% more, within an hour about 35% more and within 3 hours about 15% more.

Are there extra charges when booking on SnapPro?

No. The price in each quote is the total you pay — it already includes SnapPro's fee. You pay 25% online to book and the rest in the app after the shoot starts.
