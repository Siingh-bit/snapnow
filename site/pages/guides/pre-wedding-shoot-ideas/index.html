Pre-Wedding Shoot Ideas and Planning Tips for Indian Couples

Ideas, locations and planning tips for a pre-wedding shoot in India — themes, outfits, timing, permits and how to book a photographer.

In shortTell your story. Pick locations close together. Shoot at golden hour. Plan 2–3 outfits.

1. Tell your story

Recreate where you met, your first date café, or a hobby you share. Personal shoots photograph better than generic poses.

2. Pick locations close together

Choose 2–3 spots near each other so you don't lose the best light travelling.

3. Shoot at golden hour

The hour after sunrise and before sunset gives soft, flattering light and fewer crowds.

4. Plan 2–3 outfits

One traditional, one casual and one evening look gives variety. Bring comfortable shoes between changes.

5. Check permits

Many monuments and public places need a permit or charge a camera fee for professional shoots — ask your photographer early.

6. Book a photographer who knows your city
