Maternity Photoshoot Ideas

Maternity photoshoot ideas for Indian parents-to-be — poses, outfits, at-home and outdoor setups, and when to book.

In shortMost people book a maternity shoot between about 28 and 34 weeks (check with your doctor). Simple ideas that photograph well: window-light portraits at home, a flowing gown outdoors at golden hour, couple shots with hands on the bump, silhouettes at sunset, and family photos with older siblings.

12 ideas

1. Window-light portraits at home

Stand side-on to a big window for soft light that shows the bump beautifully — no studio needed.

2. A flowing gown at golden hour

A long gown or saree in a park or by a lake in the hour before sunset.

3. Hands on the bump

Close-ups of your hands — or both of yours — with rings or mehendi.

4. Silhouette at sunset

Stand against a bright sky so your outline shows the bump.

5. With your partner

Forehead-to-forehead, a hug from behind, or your partner kneeling to listen to the baby.

6. Older siblings

Let the big brother or sister kiss or 'talk to' the bump — candid and sweet.

7. Baby things

Tiny shoes, a scan photo or a onesie held up to the bump.

8. Traditional look

A godh bharai or baby-shower outfit with jewellery for a traditional portrait.

9. Letter boards

A board with the due month or the baby's nickname.

10. Pets

Include your dog or cat for a natural family feel.

11. Black and white

Simple dark backgrounds and black-and-white edits look timeless.

12. Your favourite place

A café, a garden or the spot you got engaged.

Planning tips

  • Pick the time of day when you have the most energy.
  • Keep a chair, water and snacks nearby.
  • Tell the photographer what you're comfortable with.
  • Book a short session — 1–2 hours is usually enough.
