6 photoshoot locations in Chandigarh

1. Rock Garden

Sculptures made from recycled material and stone pathways.

2. Sukhna Lake

A lakeside promenade with the Shivalik hills behind.

3. Zakir Hussain Rose Garden

One of Asia's largest rose gardens — best in spring.

4. Capitol Complex

Le Corbusier's modernist architecture (access is restricted; ask first).

5. Leisure Valley

Long green belts through the city.

6. Morni Hills

Hills and lakes about an hour away.

Tips for shooting in Chandigarh

  • Go early: most popular places are quieter on weekday mornings.
  • Choose 2–3 places close together so you don't lose the best light travelling.
  • October to March is the best time to shoot, with fog on some winter mornings; May and June are hot.
  • Monuments run by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and state archaeology departments usually need prior permission for professional, pre-wedding or video shoots, and some charge a camera fee. Parks, malls and private venues have their own rules. Flying a drone needs permission in many places and is not allowed near airports, military areas and many monuments.
  • Plan 2–3 outfits and keep comfortable shoes for walking between spots.

Frequently asked questions

What are the best places for a photoshoot in Chandigarh?

Popular spots include Rock Garden, Sukhna Lake, Zakir Hussain Rose Garden, Capitol Complex, Leisure Valley, and Morni Hills. The best one depends on the look you want — heritage, nature, sea or city lights.

Do I need permission for a photoshoot in Chandigarh?

Monuments run by the Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) and state archaeology departments usually need prior permission for professional, pre-wedding or video shoots, and some charge a camera fee. Parks, malls and private venues have their own rules. Flying a drone needs permission in many places and is not allowed near airports, military areas and many monuments. Your photographer can usually tell you what's needed for each place.

What is the best time for an outdoor photoshoot in Chandigarh?

The first hour after sunrise and the hour before sunset give soft, flattering light and fewer crowds. October to March is the best time to shoot, with fog on some winter mornings; May and June are hot.
