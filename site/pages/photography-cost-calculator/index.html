Frequently asked questions

How is the price worked out?

It uses SnapPro's guide prices: the type of shoot and the hours set the base price, video is about 1.3× and photos + video about 1.8× the photo price, and short-notice shoots cost more (about +60% for right now, +35% within an hour, +15% within 3 hours).

Is this the final price?

No — it's a guide to help you set a budget. On SnapPro each photographer sends their own quote, and the price in a quote already includes SnapPro's fee.

Does the city change the price?

Photographers set their own prices, so quotes vary from city to city and from photographer to photographer. Post your shoot to see real quotes in your city.
